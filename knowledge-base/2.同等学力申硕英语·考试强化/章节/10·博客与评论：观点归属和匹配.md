## 博客与评论中的观点归属

博客题有正文作者和多位评论者。相同关键词可能被不同人用来支持相反主张；正确答案依赖观点归属，而不是看哪条评论与自己想法相近。本章用2023年阅读B全文与五道题，完整演示一次。来源：合订本PDF第9—11页。

以下涉及心理健康的内容仅按当年试题辨认信息，不作为当前医疗知识或个人建议。数字也按原卷叙述阅读。

## 真题正文与五条评论

World Mental Health (WMH) Day, on 10 October, is a day to raise awareness of the effects mental illness has on millions of people's lives across the world, and to help educate and inform us all.

Around the world, one in four people will have some mental illness during their lifetime. Around 450 million people are living with a mental health problem right now, making it one of the biggest health issues in the world. Yet people rarely talk about any mental health problems they have because it is humiliating.

WMH Day was first celebrated in 1992 to raise awareness of just how common mental health issues are, fight against stigma and campaign for better conditions and treatment for people who have a mental health problem. The number of people and organizations involved in celebrating WMH Day has grown and grown, and now many countries, such as Australia, even have a Mental Health Week. Each year there is a different theme. For example, in 2017 the theme was mental health in the workplace.

WMH Day encourages us to be more aware of both our own mental health and other people's. As well as looking after yourself, think about how you could support other people. For example, you could find out more about common issues such as anxiety and depression, so you will understand friends' and colleagues' problems better. You could also encourage your workplace to start a wellness program that would benefit everyone. Companies with wellness programs have found employees take 28 per cent less time off for sickness.

**Comment 1:** Spending less time online is a huge factor to reduce anxiety and depression. Also avoiding negativity could help a lot.

**Comment 2:** Celebrating the day is just the first step. Daily education matters.

**Comment 3:** This could involve chatting on social media, hearing music, browsing new ideas, attending events and so on.

**Comment 4:** It's very difficult to persuade a person that he/she has some mental problem. It's easier for them to reject all the offers of their relatives to see a doctor than to admit they have been ill.

**Comment 5:** This topic is necessary and important to discuss. Many people suffer from mental illness and it is vital to talk about it every day rather than on a special day.

词义：raise awareness提高认识；humiliating让人感到羞耻的；stigma污名；wellness program身心健康项目；negativity消极态度。Comment 3的hearing music按听音乐理解，更自然的表达是listening to music；这里不把原卷所有搭配当范句记忆。

## 文章与评论的要点概括

正文路线：这个纪念日是什么→问题普遍但人们羞于谈论→纪念日沿革与主题→兼顾自己和他人的行动。作者支持的是提高认识、减少污名并关心自己和他人，不是仅庆祝一天。

评论可压成下面五行。写草稿时用关键词即可，不要求抄表。

| 评论 | 主要主张／观察 | 能据此回答什么 | 不能擅自添加什么 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 少上网、减少消极因素 | 消极因素与文中问题有关 | 不是完全禁止互联网 |
| 2 | 纪念日只是起点，日常教育重要 | 持续努力 | 不是说纪念日毫无作用 |
| 3 | 列举交流、音乐、活动等 | 活动方式 | 不能直接补出每天教育的要求 |
| 4 | 当事人难承认，会拒绝帮助 | 不愿承认问题 | 不是评论者赞成拒绝帮助 |
| 5 | 每天讨论，而非只在特殊日子谈 | 持续讨论 | 不是否定讨论的必要性 |

其中第4条尤其容易误读：描述某些人拒绝帮助，不等于赞同拒绝；评价对象与评论者立场要分开。

## 第41题：文章数据、事实与语气分别核对

Which of the following is true about mental illness around the world?

- A. It is not as serious as people think.
- B. A majority of people suffer from it.
- C. A shameful feeling is attached to it.
- D. People are eager to understand it.

选C，because it is humiliating与a shameful feeling对应。A反向，正文强调规模与影响；B把one in four改为大多数；D把作者希望提高认识，改成大众已经迫切希望了解。愿景不是已发生事实。

本题问正文，不要把Comment 5的“重要，应讨论”拿来证明人们都愿意讨论。题目对象在材料的哪一层，先确定。

## 第42题：年份、地点与主题的对应

What can we learn about WMH Day?

- A. It was first celebrated in Australia.
- B. Millions of people celebrate it every year.
- C. Mental health in the workplace is the constant theme.
- D. It advocates care of mental health for oneself and others.

选D，正文末段both our own ... and other people's直接支持。A把澳大利亚如今有一周活动，错连成纪念日首次地点；B把受到问题影响的millions拼到每年庆祝人数，本文未给该人数；C把2017年的例子改成恒定主题，和Each year ... different冲突。

这三项都不是简单的生词陷阱，而是跨句错误拼接。遇到熟悉词组合，逐项问它在原文属于谁、哪个年份、哪种数量。

## 第43题：根据建议推断问题与结论范围

According to Comment 1, what may result in mental illness?

- A. Unfair treatment.
- B. Limited access to the Internet.
- C. Tense human relations.
- D. Negative attitude.

选D。avoiding negativity could help支持消极态度是评论所谈风险方向。B正好反写少上网的建议；A／C没有在该评论出现。题干may保留可能性，而且明确According to Comment 1；答案是在报告该评论观点，不是据一句建议建立现实医学因果。

## 第44题：人物态度与原文证据

Which comment stresses people's unwillingness to admit their mental problems?

- A. Comment 2.
- B. Comment 3.
- C. Comment 4.
- D. Comment 5.

选C。第4条reject ... than to admit ...完整体现不愿承认；第2条讲教育，第3条讲活动，第5条讲讨论频率。题干的unwillingness没有原样出现在第4条，但行为描述构成同义证据。

## 第45题：组合选项的逐项核对

Which comments call for consistent efforts to improve mental health?

- A. Comment 1 and Comment 3.
- B. Comment 2 and Comment 5.
- C. Comment 1 and Comment 4.
- D. Comment 3 and Comment 5.

选B：第2条Daily education，第5条every day rather than on a special day，共同对应consistent efforts。D中第5条虽然对，第3条并没强调持续频率；A／C同样缺少两条同时符合的依据。组合题不能有一半正确就整项通过。

## 用对比看清“反对目标”与“要求改进”

教学句：I support fewer cars, but this fee will burden low-income workers. 说话者支持减少汽车，反对的是当前收费安排。题目若问对收费的态度，应写担忧／不赞同；若问对减少汽车的目标，仍是支持。but后面的信息重要，但不自动抹去前面的支持。

再看本篇Comment 2：just the first step不是“毫无作用”，而是有效但不足，要求接续日常教育。若选项说Celebrations should be abolished，就越过了原意。

## 观点匹配题的草稿记录格式

正文一句主张、每条评论一句立场，足以处理大部分匹配。问哪条与作者一致时回正文那一句；问评论具体信息时回对应评论；问两条组合时逐条验证。无需为了每道题重新通读全部材料。

本章题库换成新博客与评论，仍先写观点归属再作答。若你的理由只有“这句话很有道理”，尚未完成考试需要的证据判断。
