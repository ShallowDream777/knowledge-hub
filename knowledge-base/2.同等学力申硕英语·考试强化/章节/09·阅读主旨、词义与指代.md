## 文章主旨、语境词义与指代的联系

这三类任务都需要把局部放回上下文，但观察范围不同。主旨问全篇主要讨论什么；词义问这个位置的词承担什么意义；指代问一个词把读者带回哪一项内容。不是一律“看前一句”，也不是一律“读全文找中心句”。

先用2022年阅读三完整练主旨与细节的区分，接着用真实词义题和指代句练局部关系。来源：合订本PDF第33—34页，原题31—35。以下为原文，整理断行。

## 真题全文：性格与运动

Psychology studies reveal that most people taking exercise will choose their preferred sport based on their personality. If someone is introverted, patient or calm, they will most likely choose tennis, golf, or a sport where they can practice and play on their own. By contrast, those individuals who are outspoken, competitive or aggressive, will prefer contact sports or team sports.

Since a very early age, children are often seen experimenting with different types of sports—soccer, basketball, baseball, swimming, tennis, etc.—until they decide to keep practicing the sport at which they are more skillful and with which they feel more comfortable. They have the character and motivation to train, practice, and even compete in this sport. Here there is a direct relationship between the individual personality and the nature of the sport of their choosing.

Interestingly, participating in a specific type of sport might also have some influence on an individual's personality. For example, we have recently seen a dramatic increase in obesity among children, teenagers, and adults in the US. This might be caused by a vast array of personal, professional, cultural, or even health problems or conditions. However, it is up to the individual to choose a much healthier regime and therefore, embark on developing a series of new personality traits, which will help him or her lose weight and become a healthier individual.

A few decades ago, a sixty-year-old individual might have been considered a very old, fragile, and even a sick person with just a few more years to live a somewhat good, conservative life. Nowadays, we have seen a dramatic change in this perception. More than ever, we are looking at healthier, stronger, and very motivated sixty, seventy, and even eighty-year-old individuals. No doubt sports have helped shape this new personality and approach adopted by the senior population. Exercise and sports most certainly bring a new meaning to their lives, particularly because they live and experience the benefits of exercising on a daily basis. Therefore, their personalities and psychological state are most likely completely different from that sixty-year-old senior stereotype a few decades ago.

词义：introverted内向的；outspoken直言的；contact sports身体接触较多的运动；regime在此为生活安排；embark on开始着手；stereotype刻板印象。本课按文章观点练阅读，不将其性格与运动归类当作对所有人的现实诊断。

## 文章结构与全文主旨

第1段：性格影响运动选择，内向与外向举例。第2段：儿童尝试后保留适合自己的运动，延续这个方向。第3段开头出现also，改变观察方向：运动也可能影响性格。第4段用老年群体变化继续说明后一方向。

全篇有两条箭头：性格→运动选择；运动→性格与生活状态。主旨必须能包住两条，而不是只说如何挑运动，或只说老年人运动。

**第31题：What is the main topic of this passage?**

- A. How to choose the right sport.
- B. The nature of sports activities.
- C. Sport and the aging society.
- D. Personality and sport.

选D。A看似对应前两段，却把说明关系变成操作指南，遗漏后半；B过宽，未说明性格；C仅对应最后一个例证。D表述不花哨，却恰好覆盖主要对象与相互关系。主旨答案不一定最长，也不一定包含最多原词。

## 第32—35题：细节理解与例证作用

**第32题：Individuals will most likely choose a type of sport that ______.**

- A. makes them feel most comfortable
- B. has fewer demands and requirements
- C. can make the most of their potentials
- D. enables them to compete with others

选A，对应第2段with which they feel more comfortable。B无依据；C“最大化潜能”比原文更强；D是某些人的偏好，不能扩大成全部个体。

**第33题：How do children practise sports at an early age according to the passage?**

- A. They keep practising a sport till they are skilled at it.
- B. They engage in a sport that their parents choose for them.
- C. They take up any sport without obvious difficulties.
- D. They try different sports till they find one suitable for them.

选D，experimenting with different types ... until ...压缩成“尝试→选择适合的”。A改成先锁定一种再练熟，顺序与过程不同；B添加父母选择；C添加任何运动都不难。

**第34题：The author mentions the increase in obesity among Americans to ______.**

- A. illustrate the effect of sports on personality
- B. analyze the causes for this social problem
- C. give some advice on a healthy lifestyle
- D. highlight the importance of psychological traits

选A。For example前的主题句就是运动也影响性格。B抓住This might be caused ...一句，却把插入说明升级成全段目的；C把说明变成建议文；D仍漏掉运动→性格的具体关系。知道“例子回接哪句话”，比看到obesity就选健康话题更可靠。

**第35题：It is stated in the last paragraph that senior people ______.**

- A. are often associated with weakness and sickness
- B. are living a more meaningful life than before
- C. have some doubt about the value of sports
- D. have seen the changes in their own personality

选B，对应bring a new meaning to their lives。A是过去刻板印象，被Nowadays转开；C与肯定运动相反；D把作者观察到的变化改成老年人自己的觉察。再次出现“词都像、主体却被换”的干扰。

## 语境词义：三种证据合用

第07章2023年felony题可再提炼一次：laws／case限定法律领域；not a big deal与extreme提供严重程度对比；把serious crime代回能保住整段冲突。它不是“找一个看起来同主题的词”。

另一个教学例子：The timetable is flexible: workers can choose when to attend. 冒号后给释义，“可选择何时参加”支持flexible为灵活的。若选uncertain（不确定的），就把可自主选择改成安排不明确；convenient（方便的）可能是结果，却不如flexible直接对应。

不认识一个比喻时，也用附近直白解释检查。2023年finding needles in a haystack紧接tough to locate，指难以找到目标，不必先背完所有习语。但若上下文没有足够线索，应保留不确定，而不是编出唯一词义。

## 指代范围：名词短语与前述观点

回到本章第2段：**They** have the character and motivation ...。They回指children，不能因为前面最近出现sports就说运动有性格与动机。

第3段：we have ... an increase in obesity ... **This** might be caused by ...。This概括肥胖增加的现象。它不是只代individual's personality，也不是代某个具体人。后面的him or her又是泛指选择健康生活的individual。

第4段：a dramatic change in **this perception**回指上一句对六十岁人群年老体弱的看法；**their lives**指senior population中的人。正确指代要能代回整个谓语：谁有生活？人；什么被改变？看法。

## 自检：全文主旨与局部信息的区别

读完后，遮住解析回答：全篇两条箭头是什么？为什么第31题A过窄而D合适？第35题A为什么抓住了过去而不是现在？This在第3段代一个名词还是一个现象？

如果只能背出D、B，没有回答这些问题，仍需回到对应段落。章末新材料会分别考主旨、词义与指代；每次先确定题目要用多大范围的上下文，再找证据。
