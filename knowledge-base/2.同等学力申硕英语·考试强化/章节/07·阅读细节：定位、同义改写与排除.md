## 阅读细节题的定位与选项核对

本章用2023年阅读一完整走一遍。先读材料，在每段旁只写一句中文功能；随后逐题找证据。不要先背下面的结论。来源：合订本PDF第4—5页；原题21—25，段落编号为本课添加。文中事实按当年试题理解，不在此作为当前治安或政策信息。

## 真题全文：包裹失窃与监控

**第1段**

The rising popularity of having goods delivered directly to one's doorstep has come with increased attention on another phenomenon: porch theft. In communities across the country, residents have expressed concern about stolen packages.

**第2段**

Now, lawmakers around the country are aiming to crack down on it. Brookhaven police has launched “Operation Plugged In”, a program that allows residents and businesses to register their private surveillance cameras with the department, so that police can request video contents if a crime occurs in a certain area. Hundreds of individuals and businesses have registered their cameras as part of the program.

**第3段**

This technology has made policing quicker and easier, said Jacob Kissel, an officer at the Brookhaven police department. “We used to go out and knock on everybody's door after a crime happened. Now we can pull up a map and there are bullet points of all of our registered residents with cameras,” he added. That allows police to simply pick up the phone and ask residents: “Can you look at your system? Is there anything that you may be able to provide us?”

**第4段**

Scholars who study surveillance and package theft raise concerns around the widespread use of technology to catch package thieves however. For instance, facial recognition technology has been known to misidentify people with darker skin tones. One research has also shown that predominantly white neighborhoods use doorbell cameras and related platforms more frequently than their non-white counterparts, and may often use these platforms to practice “racial gate keeping”, which can lead to increased policing of communities of color.

**第5段**

Lawmakers say the new laws making package theft a felony reflect the deep concerns of their constituents. But it's unclear whether residents truly feel that strongly about the issue. One Brookhaven resident said his package was stolen last year and it was not a big deal. Since the incidents last fall, package theft has not been an issue in the community, and he was surprised to hear that police were building a felony case around the thefts. “I honestly think that's a little extreme,” said Sabah Khandaker, another Brookhaven resident. “A lot of the resources go into finding someone that has stolen a package. They could be reallocated somewhere else.”

词义支架：porch theft门廊包裹盗窃；register登记；surveillance监控；misidentify误认；constituents选民；reallocate重新分配。felony暂不查，留给语境推断题。原卷个别名词搭配不够规范，如One research；理解为一项研究，不将其作为写作范式。

## 文章段意与段落作用

第1段提出问题：送货到家越来越常见，包裹被盗引起关注。having goods delivered中的goods承受配送，不是货物自己送快递。

第2段给措施：居民和商家可以登记私人摄像头，某地发生犯罪时警方可申请录像。that allows ...修饰program；so that引出设立这种登记机制的用途；if限定实际请求录像的条件。不是警方随时控制所有私人摄像头。

第3段报告警察认为有效：过去逐户敲门，现在地图查登记点，再电话询问。This technology与That指上文登记和检索的做法，不能读成“地图自动监控所有居民”。

第4段转入担忧：however提示对前述便利提出另一面。两个例子围绕肤色、社区与执法差异，不是泛泛说“科技有缺点”。注意最后which can lead to ...接出可能的后果，仍不是任何情况下必然发生。

第5段再增加居民视角：议员说选民深切担忧，但作者说程度尚不明确；有居民觉得事情不严重，另有人认为投入过度。文章安排的是“问题→措施→效率→风险→是否反应过度”，不是从头到尾宣传一种措施。

## 第21题：行为细节与原文对应

“Operation Plugged In” allows the police to ______.

- A. use the videos from registered cameras
- B. check on the residents with cameras
- C. install more surveillance cameras
- D. require residents to register their cameras

先把问题缩成“项目让警方能做什么”，定位第2段program和allows。证据链是residents ... register cameras → police can request video contents。对应A：使用登记摄像头提供的录像。

B把查看录像改成检查居民；C把登记已有私人设备改成安装更多；D把允许居民登记改成要求居民登记。三项都拿原文词汇组合，却替换了行为或强制程度。答案A并非逐词复印原文，use videos是对request video contents供查案的合理概括。

做笔记时写“第2段：登记→可请求录像；D的require无依据”，不要只写“定位allows”。定位成功还不等于完成比较。

## 第22题：根据两个例子概括段落主题

What is Paragraph 4 mainly concerned with?

- A. Violation of privacy.
- B. Racial discrimination.
- C. Abuse of technology.
- D. Policing efficiency.

选B。misidentify people with darker skin tones、white/non-white neighborhoods、racial gate keeping、communities of color共同指向种族差别影响。A是监控话题里常见的担忧，但本段没有以私人信息泄露为核心。C过宽，没抓住两个具体例子的共同主题；D是上一段的内容，被however转开。

mainly concerned问本段主要内容。即使一个宽泛选项从逻辑上能包住B，也不等于最能概括本段；应选有明确共同证据、概括范围合适的主题。

## 第23题：根据语境推断 felony 的含义

The word “felony” (Para.5) is closest in meaning to ______.

- A. minor offense
- B. dishonest behavior
- C. immoral conduct
- D. serious crime

选D。原文是laws making ... a felony、building a felony case，法律语境先排除仅作道德判断的B／C；居民又说not a big deal、a little extreme，对比显示官方按较严重犯罪处理，排除minor offense。不能看到偷包裹似乎“小事”，就把felony猜成轻罪；那是用个人判断替代词在文中的法律等级。

## 第24题：观点归属与适用范围

What does Khandaker think of the crackdown on package thefts?

- A. It will turn out rewarding.
- B. It hurts resident relationships.
- C. It seems to be an overreaction.
- D. It will achieve the intended effect.

选C。a little extreme直接对应overreaction，后句认为资源可以分配到别处提供理由。A／D都预测措施有效，原文没有这样评价；B把担忧换成邻里关系，也无证据。另一位居民说not a big deal，可帮助理解同段氛围，但本题直接证据仍是Khandaker自己的话。

## 第25题：作者语气与引述观点的区别

What is the author's tone in discussing package thefts?

- A. Ironic.
- B. Objective.
- C. Relieved.
- D. Humorous.

选B。作者交代关注、措施、警察肯定、学者担忧和居民质疑，让读者看见几种立场。I honestly think ...出自居民，不是作者直接宣告；全文没有用反讽或幽默组织表达，也没有作者松了一口气的语气。objective在这里是相对中性、平衡报道，并不意味着作者没有选择和安排材料。

## 补充练习：EXCEPT 排除题

2022年第21题问反式脂肪对身体的作用，EXCEPT：A. putting on weight；B. raising good cholesterol；C. causing heart attack；D. blocking blood vessels。证据段写：Trans fats raise bad cholesterol and **lower the good cholesterol** ... build up in the body and block blood flow to the heart。

本题选B，方向由lower反成raising。A与obesity对应，C／D与心脏风险、阻塞对应。这里是核对当年阅读材料，不用扩展到现实健康建议。先圈EXCEPT，再核对四个判断，才能避免把正确事实选成“例外”。

## 同义改写中的词语与信息对应

经过本篇，你已经见到三种常见改写：request video contents→use videos（行为概括）；a little extreme→overreaction（评价近义）；not a big deal与严厉法律处理的反差→serious crime（语境推义）。不是所有同义都靠背单词表，关系也在上下文里。

进入本章练习后，每题只须记一句精确证据和主要排除理由。若找不到证据，不要先用“太绝对”“太片面”这类评价词遮盖缺口。下一章专门处理原文没有直接照说、却要求你推断或辨认立场的题。
