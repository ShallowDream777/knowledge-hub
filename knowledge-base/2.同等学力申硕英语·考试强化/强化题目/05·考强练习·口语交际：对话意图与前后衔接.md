## 单空对话：五项

选出同时符合前后语境的回应。

**Q01**　A: Would you mind moving your bag? B: ____ I will put it under my seat.

- A. I am afraid you are late.
- B. It costs ten pounds.
- C. Not at all.
- D. Thank you for the bag.

**Q02**　A: I am sorry I sent the wrong file. B: ____ Please send the correct one now.

- A. The office closes yesterday.
- B. These things happen.
- C. Congratulations on your success.
- D. I prefer tea.

**Q03**　A: Could we meet on Tuesday instead? B: ____ I have a meeting all day.

- A. I am afraid Tuesday will not work.
- B. Tuesday would be perfect.
- C. Thanks for explaining the route.
- D. I have already read the book.

**Q04**　A: Thank you for showing me the way. B: ____

- A. I cannot agree to cancel it.
- B. That is a difficult spelling.
- C. No, it was expensive.
- D. You are welcome.

**Q05**　A: How long does the application take? B: ____ A: Then I should apply this week.

- A. Because it is popular.
- B. A blue cover.
- C. About ten working days.
- D. At the main office.

<details>
<summary>完成本组后展开答案、证据与解析（限时卷须完成全卷后再展开）</summary>

<p><strong>Q01　答案</strong></p>
<p>C. Not at all.</p>
<p>解析：后句承诺移包，表示不介意；感谢、迟到、价格均不承接请求。</p>

<p><strong>Q02　答案</strong></p>
<p>B. These things happen.</p>
<p>解析：接受道歉后提出补救；祝贺、饮品无关；The office closes yesterday把一般现在时closes与已结束的yesterday相配，时间形式也不合适。</p>

<p><strong>Q03　答案</strong></p>
<p>A. I am afraid Tuesday will not work.</p>
<p>解析：all day meeting解释不能赴约；perfect与后文冲突，其余话题无关。</p>

<p><strong>Q04　答案</strong></p>
<p>D. You are welcome.</p>
<p>解析：回应感谢；其他选项分别引入取消、拼写、价格，缺少上下文依据。</p>

<p><strong>Q05　答案</strong></p>
<p>C. About ten working days.</p>
<p>解析：how long问时长，后句据此计划；地点、原因和物品特征都答非所问。</p>
</details>

## 整段对话一：预约

选项各使用一次。

A. Would Friday morning be possible?
B. What time would suit you?
C. I will send you a confirmation.

Patient: I need to change my appointment.
Receptionist: Of course. What day do you prefer?
Patient: 【1】
Receptionist: Yes. 【2】
Patient: Ten o'clock, please.
Receptionist: Fine. 【3】

**Q06**　空1选哪一句？

**Q07**　空2选哪一句？

**Q08**　空3选哪一句？

<details>
<summary>完成本组后展开答案、证据与解析（限时卷须完成全卷后再展开）</summary>

<p><strong>Q06　答案</strong></p>
<p>A</p>
<p>解析：前面问日期，回答星期五上午；B问具体时间，C表示确认，应在后面。</p>

<p><strong>Q07　答案</strong></p>
<p>B</p>
<p>解析：下一句Ten o’clock给时间，需询问具体时间；不是另约日期或结束确认。</p>

<p><strong>Q08　答案</strong></p>
<p>C</p>
<p>解析：日期与时间确认后发送确认，完成预约过程；另两项已完成对应功能。</p>
</details>

## 整段对话二：共同学习

选项各使用一次。

A. What do you find most difficult?
B. Let us practise explaining them to each other.
C. I can bring my notes.

Lin: I am having trouble with the course.
Sam: 【1】
Lin: I understand the examples, but I cannot explain the rules.
Sam: 【2】
Lin: Good idea. Shall we meet after class?
Sam: Yes. 【3】

**Q09**　空1选哪一句？

**Q10**　空2选哪一句？

**Q11**　空3选哪一句？

<details>
<summary>完成本组后展开答案、证据与解析（限时卷须完成全卷后再展开）</summary>

<p><strong>Q09　答案</strong></p>
<p>A</p>
<p>解析：后句解释具体困难，空白处应追问困难。</p>

<p><strong>Q10　答案</strong></p>
<p>B</p>
<p>解析：them承接rules，建议互相解释；Good idea作接受回应。</p>

<p><strong>Q11　答案</strong></p>
<p>C</p>
<p>解析：承接课后复习安排，带笔记是具体准备；不再重复追问已明确的困难。</p>
</details>

## 独立验收｜整段对话三：访谈

选项各使用一次。

A. What first interested you in this work?
B. Did you receive any training?
C. How do you deal with disagreement?
D. What do you hope to do next?

Interviewer: 【1】
Volunteer: I wanted to help older neighbours use online services.
Interviewer: 【2】
Volunteer: Yes. The library taught us how to explain each step clearly.
Interviewer: 【3】
Volunteer: I listen first and ask people to describe their problem.
Interviewer: 【4】
Volunteer: I would like to train more volunteers.

**Q12**　空1选哪一句？

**Q13**　空2选哪一句？

**Q14**　空3选哪一句？

**Q15**　空4选哪一句？

<details>
<summary>完成本组后展开答案、证据与解析（限时卷须完成全卷后再展开）</summary>

<p><strong>Q12　答案</strong></p>
<p>A</p>
<p>解析：回答说明最初动机；不是培训经历。</p>

<p><strong>Q13　答案</strong></p>
<p>B</p>
<p>解析：Yes和taught us对应是否受训。</p>

<p><strong>Q14　答案</strong></p>
<p>C</p>
<p>解析：回答描述处理分歧的方式，不是未来打算。</p>

<p><strong>Q15　答案</strong></p>
<p>D</p>
<p>解析：would like和train more volunteers回答未来希望。</p>
</details>
