## 文章一：遮阴试点与证据边界

During a hot summer, a local council installed temporary shade structures beside three playgrounds. Residents had reported that the metal seats became uncomfortable around midday. The structures were inexpensive enough to be removed if the trial failed.

Two weeks later, volunteers counted more visitors during the shaded hours than during the same hours earlier in the summer. Several parents said they could now stay longer with their children. The council described these observations as encouraging, but a researcher advising the project urged caution.

The researcher pointed out that the weather had also become cooler. In addition, a new school holiday programme had begun nearby. Without comparable information from playgrounds lacking shade, it was difficult to separate the effects of the structures from these other changes. This did not mean the shade had no value; it meant that a stronger claim needed stronger evidence.

For the next stage, the council planned to count visitors at similar playgrounds with and without shade. It also wanted to ask residents who did not use the spaces what prevented them from coming. A parent welcomed this approach, noting that comfortable seats would not help families who could not reach the playground safely.

The trial illustrates a useful habit in public decision-making: respond to practical problems, but distinguish promising observations from a complete explanation of their causes.

词语：shade遮阴；comparable可比较的；distinguish区分。

**Q01**　What can reasonably be inferred from the increase in visits?

- A. Shade was proved to be the only cause.
- B. The council had solved every access problem.
- C. Cooler weather made shade completely useless.
- D. Several changes may have contributed to it.

**Q02**　The researcher’s attitude toward the initial conclusion is best described as what?

- A. Completely convinced.
- B. Uninterested in evidence.
- C. Cautious.
- D. Hostile to all public projects.

**Q03**　Why is the nearby holiday programme mentioned?

- A. To explain the cost of the structures.
- B. To offer an alternative explanation for visitor numbers.
- C. To prove that shade frightened children.
- D. To advertise a new school.

**Q04**　What does the parent’s final comment suggest?

- A. Benefits may depend on other practical conditions.
- B. Safe routes matter less than attractive seats.
- C. No family was interested in shade.
- D. Only parents can assess research.

**Q05**　What is the author’s main evaluative position?

- A. Councils should never try inexpensive ideas.
- B. Any increase in use proves a project’s success.
- C. Residents’ experiences should always be ignored.
- D. Practical action should be accompanied by careful interpretation.

<p><strong>Q01　答案</strong></p>
<p>D. Several changes may have contributed to it.</p>
<p>解析：第3段列天气和假期项目，不能排除其他解释；only、every及completely均超出证据。</p>

<p><strong>Q02　答案</strong></p>
<p>C. Cautious.</p>
<p>解析：要求更强证据并明确不等于否定价值；不是敌视、完全确信或漠视证据。</p>

<p><strong>Q03　答案</strong></p>
<p>B. To offer an alternative explanation for visitor numbers.</p>
<p>解析：它与天气共同提示潜在混杂因素；儿童害怕、广告和结构成本都不是段落作用。</p>

<p><strong>Q04　答案</strong></p>
<p>A. Benefits may depend on other practical conditions.</p>
<p>解析：到达安全性是获得舒适座椅好处的另一条件；其余倒置比较、全否定或排他推断。</p>

<p><strong>Q05　答案</strong></p>
<p>D. Practical action should be accompanied by careful interpretation.</p>
<p>解析：结尾同时肯定回应问题和区分观察与原因；其余三项均把平衡立场改成绝对。</p>
</details>

## 推断与作用微练：六项

每题先独立分析，再用完整中文或英文作答。题中的限定信息也要保留。

**Q06**　原文“The pilot attracted 30 volunteers; a larger test is planned”，能否选“方案已经被证明适用于全国”？

**Q07**　作者写“promising, but too early for firm conclusions”，态度是全盘反对还是谨慎认可？

**Q08**　段首说“规定会造成意外负担”，后举一位老人不会用手机缴费的例子，例子的作用是什么？

**Q09**　A公司称工具革命性；作者说缺独立测试。能把revolutionary直接归为作者态度吗？

**Q10**　原文“members who attended more sessions improved more”，能证明多参加必然导致提高吗？

**Q11**　原文说“The figures should not be dismissed, but their limitations matter”，作者是否认为数据毫无价值？

<p><strong>Q06　答案</strong></p>

## 独立验收｜文章二：反馈工具与教师判断

A college tested a writing tool that highlighted repeated words and unusually long sentences. Students could use it before showing a draft to their teacher. The tool did not grade arguments or decide whether evidence was convincing.

Some students welcomed the immediate response. They could correct simple problems without waiting a week for a tutorial. Others complained that a long sentence sometimes expressed a relationship more clearly than several short ones. The teacher responsible for the trial agreed: a warning was an invitation to think, not an order to delete.

To make this distinction visible, she showed the class two versions of the same paragraph. The first had short, accurate sentences but no clear connection between them. The second contained a longer sentence explaining why one event followed another. The tool flagged the second version, yet the class found it easier to understand.

At the end of the trial, the teacher recommended keeping the tool as an aid to revision. She opposed using the number of warnings as a score for writing quality. Students still needed to consider purpose, audience and the relationship between ideas.

The lesson was not that automatic feedback was worthless. It was that useful information becomes misleading when treated as a complete judgment. A device that notices a feature cannot, by that fact alone, explain what the writer ought to do about it.

词语：draft草稿；flag标记；revision修改。

**Q12**　What can be inferred about the tool?

- A. Its warnings identify features rather than settle every writing decision.
- B. Its warnings assess the logical links between ideas in a draft.
- C. It measures clarity by counting how many warnings a revision removes.
- D. It identifies which long sentences should be split before a tutorial.

**Q13**　Why does the teacher compare two paragraphs?

- A. To identify the sentence length the tool should accept as normal.
- B. To show how immediate feedback can reduce the need for tutorials.
- C. To demonstrate how stronger arguments result from shorter sentences.
- D. To show that short sentences alone do not ensure clarity.

**Q14**　Which best describes the teacher’s position?

- A. Keeping the tool while treating shorter sentences as the clearer choice.
- B. Postponing use until the tool can judge arguments and evidence.
- C. Support for a limited use of the tool.
- D. Using warning totals as a starting score for writing quality.

**Q15**　The phrase an invitation to think suggests that students should do what?

- A. Ask the tool to supply more convincing evidence for an argument.
- B. Examine whether a warning is relevant to their intended meaning.
- C. Shorten a flagged sentence before asking why it was marked.
- D. Compare warning totals to decide which draft is clearer.

**Q16**　Which conclusion is consistent with the last paragraph?

- A. A useful measure can mislead when taken as a complete assessment.
- B. A measure becomes reliable once students learn to apply it consistently.
- C. Better revision depends on reducing the features a tool can identify.
- D. Class preference should settle disagreements about automatic warnings.

<details>
<summary>完成本组后展开答案、证据与解析（限时卷须完成全卷后再展开）</summary>

<p><strong>Q12　答案</strong></p>
<p>A. Its warnings identify features rather than settle every writing decision.</p>
<p>解析：正确依据：第1段限定工具标记重复词和长句，第3段又显示被标记的长句反而更清楚，故标记特征不等于决定怎样修改。</p>
<p>‘assess the logical links’把教师和读者的判断交给工具，超出其功能；‘counting warnings’把警告数当清晰度指标，正是教师反对的做法；‘should be split’把长句警告当拆句指令，与an invitation to think冲突。</p>

<p><strong>Q13　答案</strong></p>
<p>D. To show that short sentences alone do not ensure clarity.</p>
<p>解析：正确依据：短句版准确却缺少连接，较长版解释因果，班级认为后者更易懂。比较说明短并不自动清楚。</p>
<p>例子未设定正常长度阈值；及时反馈省去等待是前段学生谈的好处，不是两段比较的目的；‘stronger arguments’把清晰度换成论据质量，且‘shorter’与该例结果相反。</p>

<p><strong>Q14　答案</strong></p>
<p>C. Support for a limited use of the tool.</p>
<p>解析：正确依据：第4段同时有keeping the tool as an aid和反对警告数评分，构成有边界的支持。</p>
<p>‘starting score’仍把警告数用作写作质量分数，与opposed相反；‘shorter ... clearer’抹去了教师用长句说明关系的反例；‘postponing use’把当前保留使用改成等待功能升级，文中没有这种计划。</p>

<p><strong>Q15　答案</strong></p>
<p>B. Examine whether a warning is relevant to their intended meaning.</p>
<p>解析：正确依据：教师把warning与order to delete区分，要求结合表达意图判断警告是否需要处理。</p>
<p>先缩短再问原因仍将警告当指令；按总数选择草稿重复了被反对的评分办法；让工具提供论据超出其已说明的标记功能。</p>

<p><strong>Q16　答案</strong></p>
<p>A. A useful measure can mislead when taken as a complete assessment.</p>
<p>解析：正确依据：useful information becomes misleading when treated as a complete judgment概括工具信息与完整判断的区别。</p>
<p>使用一致并不补足工具没有评价的内容；减少可标记特征不一定改善意义，长句例子就是反证；班级觉得某段清楚只是一个例证，没有把班级偏好设成最终裁定规则。</p>
</details>
