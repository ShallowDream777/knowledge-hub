## 文章一：种子共享与长期条件

A neighbourhood group began a seed library in a small cupboard beside a public hall. People could take a few packets for their gardens and were encouraged to return seeds from plants they later grew. The aim was not to create a commercial seed shop, but to help residents exchange useful varieties and gardening experience.

At first, donations arrived in envelopes with little information. Volunteers often could not tell when the seeds had been collected or whether they suited the local climate. Rather than reject every unlabelled packet, the group created a simple form asking for the plant name, collection date and growing conditions. This made the collection easier to use.

The organisers also discovered that popularity was not the only useful quality. A vegetable that produced less in a wet summer might survive a dry season better than a fast-growing alternative. They therefore kept some varieties that were less productive but more resilient. Visitors were encouraged to ask why a seed had been saved, not merely how many plants it could produce.

The cupboard needed regular care. Seeds had to be kept dry, old packets checked, and labels corrected. Sharing worked because volunteers did this quiet work and gardeners returned information as well as seeds. Without such effort, an attractive cupboard could quickly become a collection of mysteries.

词语：variety品种；productive高产的。词义题的目标词不提前给释义。

**Q01**　Which title best covers the passage?

- A. Why Every Garden Must Grow the Same Plant.
- B. The History of the Public Hall.
- C. What Makes a Seed Library Useful and Sustainable.
- D. How to Open a Commercial Seed Shop.

**Q02**　What does This in This made the collection easier to use refer to?

- A. Selling all unlabelled packets.
- B. Introducing a form with information about the seeds.
- C. Rejecting every donation.
- D. Moving the public hall.

**Q03**　In context, resilient most nearly means what?

- A. Able to cope with difficult conditions.
- B. Certain to produce the largest crop.
- C. Available only in commercial shops.
- D. Difficult to identify by name.

**Q04**　The collection of mysteries most likely refers to what?

- A. A set of detective novels.
- B. A secret business hidden in the hall.
- C. Plants that cannot exist in dry weather.
- D. Seeds whose identity or growing requirements are unclear.

**Q05**　Which statement reflects the passage as a whole?

- A. Volunteers should accept no donations.
- B. Once a cupboard is installed, no further effort is needed.
- C. Sharing objects may require sharing information and maintenance work.
- D. Popularity is the only basis for choosing seeds.

## 独立验收｜文章二：日程之间的空隙

When a small design company moved its meetings online, employees initially filled the time between them with more appointments. Travelling between rooms was no longer necessary, so a calendar could look impressively efficient: one video call ended at ten and the next began at the same moment.

Within weeks, however, workers reported difficulty remembering decisions. Some began the next call still thinking about the previous one. A manager responded by asking teams to leave a short buffer between meetings. The interval was not intended for another task. People could write down decisions, prepare a question, or simply stop looking at a screen.

The change reduced the number of meetings that could fit into a day. This initially worried managers who counted appointments as evidence of activity. Yet several teams found that clearer notes and fewer repeated discussions helped them finish projects on time. A full calendar, they realised, was not the same as a useful day.

The company did not ban long meetings or require everyone to take breaks at identical times. Instead, it asked teams to discuss which pauses helped their particular work. The aim was to protect attention rather than enforce a new ritual. A gap that looks empty on a timetable may still perform an important function.

词语：interval间隔；ritual固定仪式。词义题的目标词不提前给释义。

**Q12**　What is the main point of the passage?

- A. Online meetings improve work by removing the need to travel.
- B. Shortening meetings helps employees record decisions more clearly.
- C. Uniform break routines help teams use busy calendars efficiently.
- D. Time between tasks can support effective work.

**Q13**　Buffer in Paragraph 2 most nearly means what?

- A. A gap caused by a delay in starting the next call.
- B. A period spent travelling between two meeting rooms.
- C. A short protected interval.
- D. A slot reserved for finishing an unfinished assignment.

**Q14**　This initially worried managers refers to what?

- A. Asking teams to choose pauses that suited their particular work.
- B. Reducing the number of meetings that fitted into a day.
- C. Moving the company's meetings online.
- D. Workers beginning a call while still thinking about the previous one.

**Q15**　The contrast between a full calendar and a useful day supports which idea?

- A. Visible activity and effective results are not identical.
- B. Meeting notes provide a reliable count of productive hours.
- C. Successful teams fit preparation into meetings already scheduled.
- D. Meeting frequency increased after teams began recording decisions.

**Q16**　Which title is most appropriate?

- A. Keeping Online Meetings on Schedule.
- B. Using Meeting Counts to Measure Team Progress.
- C. Writing Clearer Notes to Avoid Long Discussions.
- D. The Value of Leaving Space Between Meetings.

<details>
<summary>完成本组后展开答案、证据与解析（限时卷须完成全卷后再展开）</summary>

<p><strong>Q12　答案</strong></p>
<p>D. Time between tasks can support effective work.</p>
<p>解析：正确依据：全篇从无缝排会造成的问题写到预留间隔的作用，末段又解释看似空白也有功能。</p>
<p>取消路程是最初塞满日程的背景，不能概括后续调整；措施是会议之间留空，不是把会议本身缩短；公司让各团队讨论适合自己的停顿，而非统一休息规则。</p>

<p><strong>Q13　答案</strong></p>
<p>C. A short protected interval.</p>
<p>解析：正确依据：紧接的The interval及记决策、准备问题、离屏等用途说明buffer是有意保留的短暂间隔。</p>
<p>它not intended for another task，不是赶另一项未完作业；安排是主动留空，不是下场迟开造成的延误；会议已线上化，往返房间是之前被省去的路程。</p>

<p><strong>Q14　答案</strong></p>
<p>B. Reducing the number of meetings that fitted into a day.</p>
<p>解析：正确依据：This承接紧邻的The change reduced the number of meetings ...，管理者又把会面数量当作活动证据，因此担心。</p>
<p>线上迁移是首段背景；注意力仍留在上场会议是第二段员工遇到的问题；由团队决定停顿是末段安排。后三项都在文中出现，却不是本处回指的事件。</p>

<p><strong>Q15　答案</strong></p>
<p>A. Visible activity and effective results are not identical.</p>
<p>解析：正确依据：较少会议仍能因笔记更清楚、重复讨论更少而按时完成项目，说明可见忙碌与有效成果不同。</p>
<p>笔记帮助工作，但文中没有用它计算生产时数；准备发生在预留间隔，不是塞回既有会议；会议可容纳数量降低，并未因记笔记而增加频次。</p>

<p><strong>Q16　答案</strong></p>
<p>D. The Value of Leaving Space Between Meetings.</p>
<p>解析：正确依据：标题需覆盖起初无缝排会的问题、留空措施和保护注意力的价值。</p>
<p>‘on schedule’把主题缩成准时开会；‘meeting counts’把被修正的评价方式当主旨；‘clearer notes’是留空的一项收益，但遗漏准备、休息和因团队而异的安排，范围偏窄。</p>
</details>
