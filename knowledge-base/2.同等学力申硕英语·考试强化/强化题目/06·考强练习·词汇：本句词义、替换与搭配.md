## 语境替换：分项练习15题

目标词已经在每题指明。先判断它在本句的意思，再把候选真正代回。

**Q01**　Only eligible applicants may join the programme. 选择eligible在本句的近义词。

- A. wealthy
- B. qualified
- C. available
- D. careless

**Q02**　The team will assess the environmental impact. 替换assess。

- A. evaluate
- B. ignore
- C. produce
- D. delay

**Q03**　Periodic inspections are required. 替换Periodic。

- A. Constant
- B. Accidental
- C. Final
- D. Regular

**Q04**　The old documents are fragile. 替换fragile。

- A. modern
- B. lengthy
- C. delicate
- D. valuable

**Q05**　The proposal is feasible with the current budget. 替换feasible。

- A. compulsory
- B. practical
- C. permanent
- D. famous

**Q06**　The report revealed a serious weakness. 替换revealed。

- A. disclosed
- B. concealed
- C. created
- D. repaired

**Q07**　Residents were reluctant to accept the change. 替换reluctant。

- A. unable
- B. forbidden
- C. delighted
- D. unwilling

**Q08**　The course helps learners acquire practical skills. 替换acquire。

- A. forget
- B. donate
- C. obtain
- D. require

**Q09**　The organisation aims to retain experienced staff. 替换retain。

- A. retrain
- B. keep
- C. replace
- D. dismiss

**Q10**　The benefit gradually diminished. 替换diminished。

- A. decreased
- B. vanished immediately
- C. remained unchanged
- D. became certain

**Q11**　The team maintained the service during the repairs. 替换maintained。

- A. suspended
- B. abandoned
- C. advertised
- D. sustained

**Q12**　The new design is appealing to young users. 替换appealing。

- A. compulsory
- B. familiar
- C. attractive
- D. frightening

**Q13**　The available evidence is adequate for this decision. 替换adequate。

- A. unreliable
- B. sufficient
- C. excessive
- D. irrelevant

**Q14**　Prior experience is not required. 替换Prior。

- A. Previous
- B. Future
- C. Shared
- D. Unusual

**Q15**　The delay was due to a shortage of staff. due to最接近哪项？

- A. followed by
- B. despite
- C. in addition to
- D. caused by

<details>
<summary>完成本组后展开答案、证据与解析（限时卷须完成全卷后再展开）</summary>

<p><strong>Q01　答案</strong></p>
<p>B. qualified</p>
<p>解析：eligible说符合申请资格；available有空或可用，careless粗心，wealthy富有，都不是资格条件。</p>

<p><strong>Q02　答案</strong></p>
<p>A. evaluate</p>
<p>解析：评估影响，而不是忽略、产生或推迟影响。</p>

<p><strong>Q03　答案</strong></p>
<p>D. Regular</p>
<p>解析：定期与regular对应；constant是不间断，accidental偶然，final最终。</p>

<p><strong>Q04　答案</strong></p>
<p>C. delicate</p>
<p>解析：容易损坏接近delicate；不自动等于有价值、现代或很长。</p>

<p><strong>Q05　答案</strong></p>
<p>B. practical</p>
<p>解析：现有预算下可行；永久、著名、强制均改变判断方向。</p>

<p><strong>Q06　答案</strong></p>
<p>A. disclosed</p>
<p>解析：揭示已有弱点；conceal隐藏，create制造，repair修复。</p>

<p><strong>Q07　答案</strong></p>
<p>D. unwilling</p>
<p>解析：不情愿是意愿，不等于无能力、被禁止或高兴。</p>

<p><strong>Q08　答案</strong></p>
<p>C. obtain</p>
<p>解析：获得技能；require是需要或要求，形近不能替换。</p>

<p><strong>Q09　答案</strong></p>
<p>B. keep</p>
<p>解析：保留员工；replace替换，dismiss解雇，retrain重新培训。</p>

<p><strong>Q10　答案</strong></p>
<p>A. decreased</p>
<p>解析：逐渐减少不等于立即消失；其余是保持或确定性，均不对应。</p>

<p><strong>Q11　答案</strong></p>
<p>D. sustained</p>
<p>解析：维修时维持服务；suspend暂停，abandon放弃，advertise宣传。</p>

<p><strong>Q12　答案</strong></p>
<p>C. attractive</p>
<p>解析：有吸引力；不能与形近的appalling混同，其他词不表达吸引。</p>

<p><strong>Q13　答案</strong></p>
<p>B. sufficient</p>
<p>解析：对决定足够；过量、无关和不可靠均是不同判断。</p>

<p><strong>Q14　答案</strong></p>
<p>A. Previous</p>
<p>解析：先前经验；不是未来、共同或特殊经验。</p>

<p><strong>Q15　答案</strong></p>
<p>D. caused by</p>
<p>解析：表原因；followed by只是先后，despite让步，in addition to递进。</p>
</details>

## 词汇填空：分项练习5题

大纲也允许词汇部分采用选词填空。以下空白要同时满足结构、搭配与上下文意义。

**Q16**　The launch had to wait because the final safety checks were not yet ____.

- A. public
- B. similar
- C. completed
- D. optional

**Q17**　The service accepts every resident, ____ age or income.

- A. apart from
- B. regardless of
- C. because of
- D. instead of

**Q18**　The report shows an association, but it does not ____ that one change caused the other.

- A. prove
- B. prefer
- C. borrow
- D. purchase

**Q19**　The centre needs a ____ supply of trained staff, not occasional help.

- A. brief
- B. rare
- C. reluctant
- D. steady

**Q20**　Learners requested an evening session. The centre added one ____ their feedback.

- A. in contrast to
- B. in spite of
- C. in response to
- D. in place of

<details>
<summary>完成本组后展开答案、证据与解析（限时卷须完成全卷后再展开）</summary>

<p><strong>Q16　答案</strong></p>
<p>C. completed</p>
<p>解析：检查尚未完成是继续等待的原因；是否可选、公开或相似不表达完成状态。</p>

<p><strong>Q17　答案</strong></p>
<p>B. regardless of</p>
<p>解析：every resident与不分年龄收入一致；因为、代替、排除都会改变范围。</p>

<p><strong>Q18　答案</strong></p>
<p>A. prove</p>
<p>解析：相关不等于证明因果；偏好、借用、购买都不适合that因果内容。</p>

<p><strong>Q19　答案</strong></p>
<p>D. steady</p>
<p>解析：not occasional形成持续稳定与偶尔的对比；短暂、稀少、不情愿不合供给要求。</p>

<p><strong>Q20　答案</strong></p>
<p>C. in response to</p>
<p>解析：新增晚课回应同一请求；不是取代反馈、与反馈相反或不顾反馈。</p>
</details>

## 独立验收：替换与填空混合十题

合上正文和前两组解析。前五项检查替换，后五项检查空白选择。

**Q21**　The system is intended to reduce waste. intended to接近哪项？

- A. required not to
- B. designed to
- C. proven to
- D. unable to

**Q22**　The policy had a substantial effect, though problems remained. 替换substantial。

- A. considerable
- B. minor
- C. accidental
- D. temporary

**Q23**　The campaign seeks to raise awareness. 本句raise接近哪项？

- A. rise
- B. borrow
- C. conceal
- D. increase

**Q24**　Higher demand resulted in longer waiting times. 改写resulted in。

- A. prevented
- B. was identical to
- C. led to
- D. came from

**Q25**　The editor called for a thorough review. 替换thorough。

- A. superficial
- B. comprehensive
- C. rapid
- D. occasional

**Q26**　Visitors may use the equipment only after registration; registration is therefore a ____ for access.

- A. requirement
- B. consequence
- C. reward
- D. prediction

**Q27**　The two surveys used different methods, so their figures cannot be ____ directly.

- A. forgotten
- B. advertised
- C. owned
- D. compared

**Q28**　A clear label may ____ confusion by showing users where to begin.

- A. conceal
- B. justify
- C. reduce
- D. create

**Q29**　The timetable is a proposal rather than a final decision; it remains open to ____.

- A. ownership
- B. revision
- C. punishment
- D. payment

**Q30**　The scheme can work ____ there are enough trained volunteers; without them it must stop.

- A. provided that
- B. although
- C. as though
- D. in order that

<details>
<summary>完成本组后展开答案、证据与解析（限时卷须完成全卷后再展开）</summary>

<p><strong>Q21　答案</strong></p>
<p>B. designed to</p>
<p>解析：目的或设计意图，不保证已证明有效；不能变成无能力或禁止。</p>

<p><strong>Q22　答案</strong></p>
<p>A. considerable</p>
<p>解析：效果可观但不保证完全解决；小、偶然和临时分别改变程度、方式、时间。</p>

<p><strong>Q23　答案</strong></p>
<p>D. increase</p>
<p>解析：及物raise提高意识；rise通常不及物，不能直接接awareness，borrow和conceal意义不合。</p>

<p><strong>Q24　答案</strong></p>
<p>C. led to</p>
<p>解析：需求高导致等待长；came from会反转原因结果，prevented相反，identical不是因果。</p>

<p><strong>Q25　答案</strong></p>
<p>B. comprehensive</p>
<p>解析：全面彻底；快速或偶尔不是同一维度，superficial表面肤浅是反方向。</p>

<p><strong>Q26　答案</strong></p>
<p>A. requirement</p>
<p>解析：only after给进入条件，故是要求；不是进入后的结果、奖励或预测。</p>

<p><strong>Q27　答案</strong></p>
<p>D. compared</p>
<p>解析：方法不同限制直接比较；忘记、宣传、拥有不表达数据可比性。</p>

<p><strong>Q28　答案</strong></p>
<p>C. reduce</p>
<p>解析：明确起点是减少困惑的机制；创造、掩藏、为困惑辩解不合解释。</p>

<p><strong>Q29　答案</strong></p>
<p>B. revision</p>
<p>解析：proposal与非最终状态允许修改；惩罚、付款、所有权都不对应对未定方案作修改。</p>

<p><strong>Q30　答案</strong></p>
<p>A. provided that</p>
<p>解析：后半without ... stop明确条件；不是虽然、仿佛或为了某目的。</p>
</details>

## 验收与复测

独立验收范围：Q21、Q22、Q23、Q24、Q25、Q26、Q27、Q28、Q29、Q30。

卷面得分之外，另标能否说明本句依据：猜对而解释不出的题标“不稳”，不抹去实际答对记录。错题重新代入候选，写明它改了动作、程度、因果、条件还是词性，并选一个最接近的错误项说明区别。达到分数后，仍须对错题和不稳题完成解释再继续。
