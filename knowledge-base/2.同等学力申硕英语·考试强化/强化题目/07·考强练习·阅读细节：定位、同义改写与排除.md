## 文章一：图书馆预约试点

A city library used to provide computer help only during normal opening hours. Staff noticed that many requests came from people who worked during the day. These residents could reach the library only shortly before it closed, leaving little time for questions about online forms or job applications.

The library therefore introduced evening appointments on two days each week. Each appointment lasted twenty minutes. Residents booked by telephone or at the front desk; owning a computer or an email account was not required. Volunteers helped visitors understand the steps on a screen, but did not complete financial transactions on their behalf.

After an eight-week trial, the number of requests for help had increased. However, staff did not treat this as proof that the whole city's computer skills had improved. The trial counted visits, not changes in ability. Some residents had returned several times to practise the same task.

The service remained free, although visitors who missed an appointment were asked to cancel in advance next time. The library planned to keep the evening sessions, provided that enough trained volunteers were available. It would decide later whether to add a third evening.

词语：transaction交易；on their behalf代表他们；provided that只要／在……条件下。

**Q01**　Why were evening appointments introduced?

- A. To help people unavailable during the day.
- B. To reduce the number of volunteers.
- C. To make all financial services free.
- D. To replace normal opening hours.

**Q02**　How could residents book help?

- A. Only through a computer account.
- B. By asking a bank to arrange it.
- C. Without making any appointment.
- D. By telephone or at the front desk.

**Q03**　What were volunteers instructed not to do?

- A. Help people understand forms.
- B. Take part in evening sessions.
- C. Carry out financial transactions for visitors.
- D. Explain steps on a screen.

**Q04**　What does the rise in requests directly show?

- A. The trial measured changes in ability.
- B. More requests for the service were recorded.
- C. Every resident improved computer skills.
- D. No visitor needed repeated help.

**Q05**　What condition affected the continuation of evening sessions?

- A. The availability of trained volunteers.
- B. A fee paid by all visitors.
- C. The purchase of personal computers.
- D. Immediate approval of a third evening.

<p><strong>Q01　答案</strong></p>
<p>A. To help people unavailable during the day.</p>
<p>解析：第1段给白天工作、到馆太晚的困难；减少志愿者、免费金融业务和替代正常开放均未陈述。</p>

<p><strong>Q02　答案</strong></p>
<p>D. By telephone or at the front desk.</p>
<p>解析：第2段明确两个渠道，并否定必须拥有电脑或邮箱；银行和无需预约都无依据。</p>

<p><strong>Q03　答案</strong></p>
<p>C. Carry out financial transactions for visitors.</p>
<p>解析：did not complete ... on their behalf是限制；解释步骤和表格正是服务，晚间参与也没有被禁止。</p>

<p><strong>Q04　答案</strong></p>
<p>B. More requests for the service were recorded.</p>
<p>解析：第3段区分访问数与能力变化；其余三项分别扩大范围、与重复来访矛盾、颠倒统计对象。</p>

<p><strong>Q05　答案</strong></p>
<p>A. The availability of trained volunteers.</p>
<p>解析：末段provided that给条件；收费、购买电脑和立即增加第三晚均不成立。</p>

## 细节微练：六项

每题先独立分析，再用完整中文或英文作答。题中的限定信息也要保留。

**Q06**　原文“Applicants must submit the form before Friday”，选项“on or after Friday”，指出问题。

**Q07**　原文“The grant covers books but not travel”，问哪项不报销。

**Q08**　原文“The centre lowered its fees because demand fell”，费率降低的直接原因是什么？

**Q09**　原文“The experiment included adults aged 40 to 60”，能支持针对所有儿童的结论吗？

**Q10**　原文“New members may attend after registration”，选项说新成员都已经参加，是否成立？

**Q11**　原文“Only two of the five sites stayed open”，问开放地点数量。

<p>时间要求被反转。</p>

<p>解析：two是开放数，five是总数；不能看见总数就答五个。</p>

## 独立验收｜文章二：维修而非替换

A housing association received frequent complaints about broken cupboards in its older flats. Replacing every cupboard would be expensive and disruptive. Rather than ordering new units immediately, the association asked a repair team to inspect the damaged ones.

The team found that most problems involved hinges and handles, not the wooden frames. It proposed replacing small parts where possible. Residents were given a choice between morning and afternoon visits. They were not asked to move out, but someone had to be at home to let the workers in.

During the first month, repairs cost less than the amount estimated for replacement. The association also kept usable wood out of the waste stream. Yet the report did not say repair would always be cheaper. Units with badly damaged frames still needed to be replaced, and repeated visits could increase labour costs.

Some residents wanted an entirely new kitchen rather than repaired cupboards. The association explained that the project addressed safety and function, not a complete redesign. It promised a follow-up check after six months so that faults returning after the first repair could be identified.

词语：housing association住房管理机构；hinge铰链；frame框架；disruptive造成干扰的。

**Q12**　Why did the association first request inspections?

- A. To check whether faults had returned after six months.
- B. To examine whether repair could avoid complete replacement.
- C. To find which visiting hours residents preferred.
- D. To select cupboards for a complete kitchen redesign.

**Q13**　What caused most cupboard problems?

- A. Small parts such as hinges and handles.
- B. Damage to the wooden frames rather than the small fittings.
- C. Residents choosing unsuitable replacement cupboards.
- D. Repeated visits made by the repair workers.

**Q14**　What did residents need to do during a visit?

- A. Leave a key so workers could enter an empty flat.
- B. Pay the labour costs before choosing a visiting time.
- C. Arrange a frame inspection before booking the visit.
- D. Ensure someone was at home to admit the workers.

**Q15**　Which statement is supported by the report?

- A. Lower costs persuaded residents to give up requests for new kitchens.
- B. Most wooden frames were replaced before repairs began.
- C. Some cupboards still required replacement.
- D. The first month's savings excluded the cost of repeat visits.

**Q16**　Why was a six-month follow-up planned?

- A. To assess whether repaired cupboards matched a new design.
- B. To detect faults that might return.
- C. To compare the cost of morning and afternoon visits.
- D. To decide which residents should receive a new kitchen.

<details>
<summary>完成本组后展开答案、证据与解析（限时卷须完成全卷后再展开）</summary>

<p><strong>Q12　答案</strong></p>
<p>B. To examine whether repair could avoid complete replacement.</p>
<p>解析：正确依据：第1段说完整替换昂贵且干扰生活，因此先检查是否可以维修。</p>
<p>‘visiting hours’是随后安排上门的时间选择，不是最初检查的目的；‘complete kitchen redesign’是部分住户的愿望，项目并不提供；‘after six months’属于以后复查，错置了时间。</p>

<p><strong>Q13　答案</strong></p>
<p>A. Small parts such as hinges and handles.</p>
<p>解析：正确依据：第2段most problems involved hinges and handles，故问题多在小配件。</p>
<p>‘wooden frames’与not the wooden frames反向；‘residents choosing’把机构检查后的方案选择改成住户选错橱柜，文中未说；‘repeated visits’影响的是人工成本，不是原有故障的主要来源。</p>

<p><strong>Q14　答案</strong></p>
<p>D. Ensure someone was at home to admit the workers.</p>
<p>解析：正确依据：someone had to be at home to let the workers in明确要求有人在家开门。</p>
<p>‘leave a key’不能替代原文有人在家的要求；文中未规定住户先交人工费；最初检查由机构安排，也未要求住户先约框架检查才可预约维修。不能把常见维修流程当成本文规定。</p>

<p><strong>Q15　答案</strong></p>
<p>C. Some cupboards still required replacement.</p>
<p>解析：正确依据：第3段说框架严重损坏的橱柜仍需替换。</p>
<p>‘excluded the cost’无依据：报告只说重复上门可能增加人工成本，未交代首月核算是否排除了它；‘persuaded residents’把机构的解释改成住户已经改变愿望；‘most wooden frames’与问题主要在小配件不符，也没有事先大量换框架的记录。</p>

<p><strong>Q16　答案</strong></p>
<p>B. To detect faults that might return.</p>
<p>解析：正确依据：末句so that faults returning after the first repair could be identified直接交代复查目的。</p>
<p>上午下午只是预约时段，没有比较其费用；新厨房是住户提出但超出本项目的需求；项目关注安全和功能，未实施新设计，也不是验收外观是否匹配。</p>
</details>

## 验收与复测

独立验收范围：Q12、Q13、Q14、Q15、Q16。

本组5题，答对每题1分，建议首次至少4/5。卷面得分之外，另记能否指出原文依据；只凭“这项比较温和”猜对的，先标“不稳”，不抹去实际答对记录。达到分数后仍须完成下述证据解释，再继续学习。
