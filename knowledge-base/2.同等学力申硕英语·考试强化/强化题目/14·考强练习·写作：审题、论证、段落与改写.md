## 句子修复：八项

每题先独立分析，再用完整中文或英文作答。题中的限定信息也要保留。

**Q01**　改正：Lifelong learning important for workers.

**Q02**　改正：Online courses saves time for many students.

**Q03**　改正：Many people study online, they need flexible hours.

**Q04**　改正：Although the method is useful, but it has limits.

**Q05**　改正：People should to check the information before sharing it.

**Q06**　改正：This experience made me to understand the value of practice.

**Q07**　改正想表达“并非每个问题都能由技术解决”：Every problem cannot be solved by technology.

**Q08**　改正：The people which work at night need different arrangements.

<details>
<summary>完成本组后展开答案、证据与解析（限时卷须完成全卷后再展开）</summary>

<p><strong>Q01　答案</strong></p>
<p>Lifelong learning is important for workers.</p>
<p>解析：主语后缺系动词is，不能把名词短语与形容词直接拼成完整陈述句。</p>

<p><strong>Q02　答案</strong></p>
<p>Online courses save time for many students.</p>
<p>解析：courses复数，普通现在时配save；不能因后面time不可数而改主谓关系。</p>

<p><strong>Q03　答案</strong></p>
<p>Many people study online because they need flexible hours. 也可用合适标点拆句。</p>
<p>解析：两个独立分句仅用逗号连接不规范；because表达本题给出的原因。</p>

<p><strong>Q04　答案</strong></p>
<p>Although the method is useful, it has limits.</p>
<p>解析：本课标准让步句不重复使用but连接同一主从关系。</p>

<p><strong>Q05　答案</strong></p>
<p>People should check the information before sharing it.</p>
<p>解析：情态should后用原形，不带to；before sharing表达分享前。</p>

<p><strong>Q06　答案</strong></p>
<p>This experience made me understand the value of practice.</p>
<p>解析：主动make＋宾语＋原形；不能把被动was made to do的搭配直接套来。</p>

<p><strong>Q07　答案</strong></p>
<p>Not every problem can be solved by technology. 或Technology cannot solve every problem.</p>
<p>解析：把否定范围写清楚，避免原句产生全否定／部分否定的理解歧义。</p>

<p><strong>Q08　答案</strong></p>
<p>The people who/that work at night need different arrangements.</p>
<p>解析：指人的限制性定语从句主语通常用who或that，不用which；不另重复they。</p>
</details>

## 段落展开与审题：三项

写出要求的组织内容，不追求唯一措辞。

**Q09**　围绕“灵活的课程时间让学习更可行”写4句：主题、解释、例子、回扣。

**Q10**　题目讨论self-employment的利弊。列一个好处、一个风险和一个有条件结论，再写80—100词段落。

**Q11**　给定图表：某培训班线上学习者占比，2020年20%、2022年35%、2024年50%。写两句准确描述和一句有限解释。

<details>
<summary>完成本组后展开答案、证据与解析（限时卷须完成全卷后再展开）</summary>

<p><strong>Q09　答案</strong></p>
<p>Flexible course times make learning more accessible. Workers can choose sessions that fit their shifts. For example, a shop assistant may attend an evening class after work. This gives adults with fixed duties a realistic way to develop new skills.</p>
<p>解析：检查四句功能是否不同；例子须具体，不把假想个例当现实统计。</p>

<p><strong>Q10　答案</strong></p>
<p>要点示例：时间自主；收入不稳定；有储备与规划时更可行。可写：Self-employment can give people greater control over their schedules. This may help them combine paid work with family duties. However, freedom does not guarantee a stable income. A person who depends on one client may face serious difficulty when that contract ends. For this reason, preparation matters as much as enthusiasm. People considering this path should assess their likely expenses, develop useful skills and avoid relying on one source of work. With realistic planning and some financial reserves, self-employment can be a worthwhile option, but it is not equally suitable for everyone.</p>
<p>解析：示例只是一种论证；不能只列好处而漏风险，也不要把有条件结论写成适用于所有人。</p>

<p><strong>Q11　答案</strong></p>
<p>The share of learners studying online rose from 20 percent in 2020 to 50 percent in 2024. It increased by 30 percentage points over the period. The change may reflect a growing need for flexible study, though the chart alone does not establish its causes.</p>
<p>解析：图表数据是原创练习设定。保留起止年份、占比与百分点；可能解释不能当图表已证实原因。</p>
</details>

## 完整写作一：终身学习

Write at least 150 words on The Importance of Lifelong Learning. Discuss both personal and professional development, with examples and reasons.

**Q12**　先独立列提纲，再写完整文章，完成后展开示范。

<details>
<summary>完成本组后展开答案、证据与解析（限时卷须完成全卷后再展开）</summary>

<p><strong>Q12　答案</strong></p>
<p>Lifelong learning matters because neither our working lives nor our personal needs remain unchanged. Education should therefore continue beyond the classroom. It helps people respond to new demands while giving them more choices about the lives they want to lead.</p>
<p>In professional life, continued learning makes it easier to adapt. A worker who learns to use a new system may perform familiar tasks more effectively and become ready for different responsibilities. For example, an office assistant who develops basic data skills can contribute to projects that were previously beyond reach. Learning does not guarantee promotion, but it can create opportunities.</p>
<p>On a personal level, learning builds confidence and curiosity. Studying a language, exploring local history or practising a practical skill can make daily life more rewarding. It may also bring people into contact with others who share their interests.</p>
<p>To make lifelong learning realistic, people need manageable goals and regular practice. A short, consistent habit is often more useful than an ambitious plan that is soon abandoned. With this approach, learning becomes a lasting part of life rather than a temporary obligation.</p>
<p>示范词数：182。先完成自己的文章，再比较组织和语言。</p>
<p>解析：第1段回答重要性；第2段职业发展及具体例子并保留不保证晋升；第3段个人发展；第4段可行做法收束。</p>
</details>

## 完整写作二：技术与人的作用

Write at least 150 words on whether machine translation can fully replace human translators. Consider speed, accuracy and performance in context.

**Q13**　先独立列提纲，再写完整文章，完成后展开示范。

<details>
<summary>完成本组后展开答案、证据与解析（限时卷须完成全卷后再展开）</summary>

<p><strong>Q13　答案</strong></p>
<p>Machine translation has become a useful part of communication. It can produce a draft quickly and help readers understand the general meaning of an unfamiliar text. However, speed alone does not show that it can fully replace human translators.</p>
<p>For simple and repetitive material, automatic translation may save considerable time. A traveller who needs the basic meaning of a notice may prefer an immediate answer to a perfect one. Professional translators can also use an automatic draft as a starting point, provided that they check it carefully.</p>
<p>The difficulty becomes greater when meaning depends on context. A phrase may express politeness, humour or criticism rather than its most direct dictionary meaning. In such cases, an inaccurate choice can change the writer&#x27;s intention even if the sentence sounds fluent. A human translator must consider the audience, the purpose and the consequences of misunderstanding.</p>
<p>I therefore see machine translation as a powerful aid rather than a complete substitute. Its appropriate use depends on the task. The more important precision and responsibility become, the more necessary careful human judgment is likely to remain.</p>
<p>示范词数：180。先完成自己的文章，再比较组织和语言。</p>
<p>解析：明确立场；速度用具体场景解释；准确性连接语境；讨论不同任务表现与责任，避免通篇只说科技方便。</p>
</details>

## 独立验收｜完整写作三：让生活更简单

Write at least 150 words on Ways to Live a Simpler Life in a Modern World. Explain practical methods and why they may help.

**Q14**　先独立列提纲，再写完整文章，完成后展开示范。

<details>
<summary>完成本组后展开答案、证据与解析（限时卷须完成全卷后再展开）</summary>

<p><strong>Q14　答案</strong></p>
<p>Modern life offers many choices, but managing them can become a burden. Living more simply does not require rejecting technology or giving up every comfort. It means choosing activities and possessions that serve a clear purpose.</p>
<p>One practical step is to reduce unnecessary commitments. Before accepting a new task, people can ask whether it supports an important goal and whether they have enough time to do it properly. Leaving some space in a schedule can make existing responsibilities easier to handle.</p>
<p>Another step is to use technology deliberately. Turning off nonessential notifications, for example, can protect a period of study or conversation. The aim is not to avoid all messages, but to decide when they deserve attention. A similar principle applies to buying things: delaying a purchase gives us time to consider whether it will actually be useful.</p>
<p>Finally, simple routines can reduce repeated decisions. Planning a few regular study periods or preparing the next day&#x27;s essentials may save both time and effort. These small changes will not remove every difficulty, but they can help people focus on what matters most.</p>
<p>示范词数：181。先完成自己的文章，再比较组织和语言。</p>
<p>解析：建议题必须给方法与作用；拒绝把简化生活写成拒绝一切技术；例子落到时间、通知、购买和习惯。</p>
</details>
